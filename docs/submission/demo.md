## What it shows

The customer edits an order, sees the cart change, reviews the exact quote and taps **Confirm order** after voice ends. One accepted **$9.72** order contains a classic burger without onions and a medium lemonade without ice. It reaches the local POS sandbox and kitchen display.

The cart initially showed a large drink although the input clip was scripted to request medium. A further spoken clarification restored medium before confirmation. This recovery remains visible. Its acoustic cause is unresolved.

The capture passed 21 checks: no order or approval before screen confirmation, voice ending before confirmation, one order/approval/receipt, and agreement between the cart and displayed quote. Media and browser playback checks passed. Separate spoken-yes, cancellation and staff-takeover tests are outside this recording.

## Boundaries

This browser-based **Alexa+ concept simulation** uses synthetic customer speech and replies generated by Nova during the recording. Native Alexa+ devices, physical microphones, restaurant delivery and payments were not used. Media checks cover decoding and playback; naturalness and performance in noise were not evaluated.

The recording predates the concise-response prompt. The prompt has automated test coverage, but its effect on spoken reply length remains unverified.
